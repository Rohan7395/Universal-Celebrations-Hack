# BrightDays Home Care: Client & Family Handbook

*Synthetic document for the hackathon demo. BrightDays Home Care is a fictional agency.*

BrightDays Home Care is a family-owned, in-home care agency serving older adults in San Mateo County, California, since 2014. We care for about 90 Clients with a team of 34 Caregivers.

## Our services

| Service | What it includes | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Personal care visits | Bathing, dressing, mobility help, light housekeeping, meal preparation | $38/hour, 3-hour minimum |
| Companionship visits | Conversation, walks, games, errands, rides to appointments | $34/hour, 2-hour minimum |
| Daily wellbeing check-in calls | A short friendly phone call every day at a time the Client chooses | Included with any weekly visit plan |
| Medication reminder calls | A phone call at each scheduled medication time to remind the Client to take their medication and confirm it was taken | $45/month |
| Family updates | A short update to the Family Contact after each visit, and right away if anything changes | Included |
| Respite care | Overnight or weekend care so family caregivers can rest | $360 per 24 hours |

## Visits

- Visits are scheduled weekly. Each Client has a primary Caregiver, so they see a familiar face.
- To change or cancel a visit, contact us at least 24 hours before. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice are charged at 50%.
- Same-day urgent visits are available when a Client's safety requires it. We do not charge extra for urgent visits that we arrange.
- If a Caregiver is sick, we send a substitute Caregiver who has read the Client's care plan.

## Who we talk to

- We share information about a Client only with the Family Contacts listed in the Client's care plan.
- We confirm a Family Contact by the phone number on file. If someone calls from a number that is not on file, we give general information only.
- Clients can add or remove Family Contacts at any time.

## Safety

- If a Client has a fall, chest pain, trouble breathing, or talks about hurting themselves, we tell them to call 911 and we contact the on-call coordinator immediately.
- We always tell the Family Contact when something happens to their loved one, even if the Client asks us not to.
- Our staff do not give medical advice. We do not tell Clients to change, skip, or double a medication. We direct medical questions to the Client's doctor or pharmacist.
- We watch for scams. We never ask a Client for bank details, passwords, or Social Security numbers, and we tell families when someone else has.

## Things we never do

- We never promise that a cost will be covered by insurance or a government program.
- We never share a Client's address, door code, or schedule with anyone who is not a verified Family Contact.
- We never leave a Client alone in an unsafe situation to keep to a schedule.
